ALX-26-RW01 A Neighborhood Field Guide Compiled August 14, 2026

Restaurant Week at Lunch

Alexandria Summer Restaurant Week runs Friday, Aug 14 – Sunday, Aug 23 — ten days, two weekends. The headline program is prix-fixe dinner ($35/$45/$55 across 78 restaurants), but this report covers the part that matters on a work-from-home day: lunch. Every deal below was read off the venue's own posted menu or Visit Alexandria's official 76-page menu book — and the whole 88-listing roster was checked, so this is the complete lunch field, not a sampler — including two prix fixes the roster doesn’t list. Distances are real routed times from home.

The Findings

The prix-fixe lunches

The official roster shows exactly two lunch prix fixes — neighbors on the same Del Ray block, both a 15-minute walk. But menu-level digging turned up two more the roster doesn’t advertise: Rustico runs a $25 three-course lunch and weekend brunch, and Cheesetique serves its $45 Restaurant Week menu at lunch too (through Aug 30, a week past everyone else).

Worth Knowing

Notes for the week

  • No dinner menu moonlights at lunch. All 78 dinner-tier listings were checked individually — none extends its $35/$45/$55 prix fixe to lunch service. If a favorite dinner spot is open at noon, it's serving its regular menu.
  • Little Birdie's clock: its $25 lunch runs weekdays only, so there are six shots at it — today (Fri 8/14) and Mon 8/17 through Fri 8/21. Matt & Tony's works all ten days.
  • 5% back: the ALX Open Rewards app earns 5% back on Restaurant Week purchases, redeemable around town later.
  • Reservations: irrelevant for the fast-casual track; for the two prix-fixe lunches neither menu shows a reservation requirement, but Matt & Tony's weekend brunch gets busy — book or go early.

Methodology

Compiled August 14, 2026. The complete official roster (78 prix-fixe dinner listings + 10 fast-casual listings) was pulled from Visit Alexandria's restaurant-week database and every listing was checked for a lunch offering. The two prix-fixe lunch menus were transcribed from the venues' own posted menu graphics; the eight fast-casual deals were transcribed page-by-page from Visit Alexandria's official Restaurant Week menu book. Press coverage (ALXnow, Alexandria Living Magazine, The Zebra, and others) was swept and is dinner-only. A later menu-level pass over all dinner participants surfaced the two off-roster lunch options (Rustico, Cheesetique), added the same day. Walk and drive times are routed estimates from home in Rosemont / north Old Town via OpenStreetMap routing — not straight-line — so they account for the rail-crossing detours. Opening hours for fast-casual venues are aggregator-sourced and not independently re-verified; Foster's Grille and Union Sandwich Co. pages in the menu book carry no explicit dates, so the full Aug 14–23 window is inferred from the rest of the book.
