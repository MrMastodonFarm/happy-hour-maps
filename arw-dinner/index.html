ALX-26-RW02 A Neighborhood Field Guide Compiled August 14, 2026

Restaurant Week at Dinner

The main event: 77 restaurants, prix-fixe dinners at $35 / $45 / $55 per person, Friday Aug 14 – Sunday Aug 23 — full window, both weekends, no excluded nights found anywhere. 37 of the 77 are within the 27-minute walk line from home; nothing else in the city is more than a 7-minute drive. Menus below were read from each venue's own site or transcribed from Visit Alexandria's official 76-page menu book; where a menu isn't published anywhere, it says so. Reservations are per-restaurant (OpenTable is the norm), and the ALX Open Rewards app earns 5% back. Companion report: Restaurant Week at Lunch.

Worth Knowing

Notes for the week

  • No press picks exist yet — no outlet has published "best of" choices for this edition. ALXnow's announcement spotlights three first-time participants: Umiya Sushi, Pak Soii, and Dok Khao.
  • Formats worth knowing: Pak Soii runs a five-course izakaya progression; Tequila & Taco is the only menu that includes a drink (a margarita); Hank's Pasta Bar starts with a free shared antipasti platter; Sweet Fire Donna's and Pork Barrel BBQ do big platters instead of courses; Holy Cow's "$35 Summer Stack" feeds two.
  • Price flags: Mason Social is listed at $35 but its menu-book page says $55 — call first. Evening Star's own menu says $40 against a listed $45. Both flagged in their entries above.
  • Best deal in the field (subjective but defensible): Holy Cow at ~$17.50/head, Hank's Pasta Bar's four-part $35, and Rustico covering dinner and a $25 lunch/brunch. At the top end, Bastille's $55 tier (lamb chops, duck confit) and Pak Soii's five courses read like the most food for the money.
  • Cheesetique keeps going through Aug 30 — a week past the official end, at lunch and dinner both.
  • Book now for weekend nights — reservations are per-restaurant, mostly OpenTable, and the press's one consistent warning is that popular rooms fill first.

Appendix

Methodology

Compiled August 14, 2026. The complete official roster was pulled from Visit Alexandria's restaurant-week database (77 dinner participants after removing duplicates). All 77 were geocoded and routed from home in Rosemont / north Old Town via OpenStreetMap walking and driving routers — real routes, not straight lines. For the 37 walkable venues, the actual Restaurant Week menu was hunted down on the venue's own site or transcribed from the official menu book; nine venues (Lena's and its Loft, Matt & Tony's, Indochen, Namaste Jalsaa, The Majestic, Murphy's, Piece Out, The Pita House) publish no dish list anywhere and are marked accordingly. Two price discrepancies between the official listing and other sources are flagged inline rather than silently resolved. Press (ALXnow, Alexandria Living, Washingtonian, NoVA Magazine, The Zebra, Eater DC) was swept for picks and program mechanics.
